Terms

All photographs in the index are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal and will remain so. Full-resolution files are available to anyone, for any use, without attribution, permission, or an account. Support continues the fieldwork that adds to the index.

Costs

Fieldwork

Fuel, campsites, and permits for the annual regional circuits; Phase I continues through December 2032.

Archive

Raw file storage, site hosting, and the upload pipelines to Wikimedia Commons and the Internet Archive.

Fiscal sponsor

The Public Lands Institute is fiscally sponsored by CENTER, Santa Fe, a 501(c)(3) organization. Gifts are made to CENTER on behalf of the project and are tax deductible to the extent allowed by law. Check, Fidelity Charitable, Zelle, and PayPal are accepted.
