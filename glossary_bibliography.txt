Web search aggregating Britannica, USGS Geolex/pamphlets, and Kansas Geological Survey material — used for the
North American regional Mississippian series (Kinderhookian, Osagean, Meramecian) and Pennsylvanian series
(Morrowan, Desmoinesian). These regional biostratigraphic units are not part of the international (ICS) timescale
and lack a single authoritative numeric boundary; ranges given in the glossary are approximate and flagged as such.

Web search aggregating Britannica and Encyclopedia.com — used for the Leonardian (North American Lower Permian
series). Range given is approximate and flagged as such.

Cincinnatian: no single authoritative source gave a precise numeric boundary for this North American regional
series; the range in the glossary (~458-443 Mya) is an approximation for "Late Ordovician equivalent," derived
from the Ordovician period's own end boundary (443.1 Mya per ICS) and flagged as approximate in the glossary text.
